# Epholang Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Epholang is an experimental programming language built around
explicitness, readability, and strict static checking.

We welcome contributors with different backgrounds, experiences,
and levels of technical knowledge.

Our goal is to maintain a respectful, constructive, and productive
environment for everyone participating in the project.

Technical standards may be strict. Personal treatment should
remain respectful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to official Epholang community spaces,
including:

- GitHub Issues and Pull Requests.
- GitHub Discussions, if enabled.
- Official Epholang Telegram communities.
- Other official project communication channels.

It applies to contributors, users, maintainers, and moderators.

## Expected behavior

Community members are expected to:

- Treat others with respect.
- Provide constructive and informative feedback.
- Explain technical disagreements with clear reasoning.
- Respect different levels of experience.
- Stay relevant to the discussion.
- Accept constructive criticism.
- Respect other people's privacy.
- Follow applicable community and platform rules.

Humor, memes, and informal communication are welcome as long as
they do not disrupt discussions or target other participants.

## Constructive criticism

Epholang encourages open technical discussions, including
disagreement with existing language design decisions.

Criticism should focus on ideas, implementations, and technical
consequences rather than individuals.

For example:

**Constructive:**

> I think this feature introduces unnecessary complexity because
> the same behavior can already be achieved using existing syntax.

**Not constructive:**

> This feature is stupid, and anyone who designed it is an idiot.

Contributors are not required to agree with every design decision.

However, disagreements should be supported by reasoning whenever
possible.

The project maintainer has final authority over language design.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not permitted:

- Personal attacks, insults, or harassment.
- Threats or intimidation.
- Discrimination or hateful conduct.
- Bullying or deliberate humiliation.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Spam or repeated irrelevant content.
- Deliberately disrupting technical discussions.
- Repeatedly provoking conflicts without constructive intent.
- Encouraging others to engage in prohibited behavior.

Disagreement, criticism, or rejection of a proposal does not
automatically constitute harassment.

## Off-topic discussions

Discussions should remain relevant to Epholang and its community.

Political, religious, and other potentially controversial topics
are not prohibited solely because of their subject matter.

However, such discussions should have a clear connection to the
project and must follow the same standards of respectful behavior.

Moderators may redirect or close discussions that become
unproductive or unrelated to Epholang.

## Enforcement

The project maintainer is responsible for enforcing this Code
of Conduct.

Additional moderators may be appointed in the future.

Moderation decisions will consider the severity, context, and
history of the behavior.

### Minor violations

Minor violations will generally result in a reminder or warning.

Participants should normally have an opportunity to correct
their behavior.

### Repeated violations

Repeated violations may result in:

- Formal warnings.
- Removal or hiding of inappropriate comments.
- Closing disruptive discussions.
- Temporary restrictions on participation, where supported.

### Serious violations

Serious violations, including threats, severe harassment, or
deliberate disclosure of private information, may result in
immediate action without a prior warning.

Possible measures include:

- Removal of harmful content.
- Temporary or permanent restrictions from project spaces.
- Reporting violations to the relevant platform.

Moderation actions are limited to the permissions and tools
available to project maintainers.

## Reporting violations

If you encounter behavior that violates this Code of Conduct,
you may use the reporting and moderation tools provided by
the platform where the incident occurred.

For GitHub-related abuse, use GitHub's built-in reporting tools.

For project-level concerns that do not involve sensitive
information, you may contact the project maintainer through
the repository's public communication channels.

Do not publish sensitive personal information or private
evidence in public Issues or discussions.

Additional private reporting options may be introduced in
the future.

## Privacy and confidentiality

Reports will be handled with discretion.

The maintainer and any appointed moderators should avoid
unnecessary disclosure of personal information.

However, absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed,
especially when a report requires action by a third-party platform.

When appropriate, moderation decisions may be communicated
publicly without unnecessarily disclosing private information.

## Appeals

Participants may request reconsideration of a moderation
decision.

Appeals should explain why the decision may have been incorrect
or why reconsideration is appropriate.

Appeals must remain respectful and must not be used to harass
moderators or other participants.

The project maintainer makes the final decision on appeals.

## Changes to this Code of Conduct

This document may be updated as the Epholang community grows.

Significant changes should be documented and communicated
through the project's official channels.

The project maintainer has final authority over changes
to this Code of Conduct.

## Acknowledgments

Thank you for helping maintain a respectful and productive
Epholang community.
